Share Your Experiment

Ready to show your work? A live, shareable link takes just seconds — the AI handles all the git plumbing behind the scenes, so you never have to think about it.

Sharing your work
1
Share your experiment When you’re ready to share, just paste this one prompt. The AI makes a personal copy, publishes it, and hands you a link — every git step happens for you.
"Share my experiment so anyone can view it, then tell me in plain, non-technical language what you did and give me the shareable link."
The AI will automatically:
  • Create a personal copy of the project on your GitHub (a “fork”)
  • Push your experiment to it
  • Enable a live preview site (GitHub Pages)
  • Give you a shareable URL that anyone in the org can view
The first share may take 1–2 minutes for GitHub Pages to build. After that, updates are near-instant.
2
Update your shared experiment Made more changes? Push again — your link stays the same and the live preview updates on its own.
"Push my latest changes to my shared link."
Contributing back
3
Add your experiment to the main playground Want your prototype on the official Prototypes page so everyone can see it? Ask the AI to open a pull request — it handles the branching, commit, and PR for you.
"Raise a PR to add my experiment to the main prototypes page, then tell me in plain language what happens next."
A maintainer reviews your PR, may suggest changes, and merges it — then it’s available to everyone using the playground.
4
Contribute new components Built a handy UI pattern along the way? Add it back to the design system so the whole team can use it too.
"I've built a notification toast in myExperiment. Add it as a new component to the design system with a token file, preview, and catalog entry."
